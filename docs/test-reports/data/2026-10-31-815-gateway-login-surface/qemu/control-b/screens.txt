Screen dumps of boot control-b (run 2, without usbcore.authorized_default=0), transcribed by hand from the PNG
files (QEMU monitor `screendump`, 800x480). The PNG files are not in the repository. Status markers keep their
text, colours are left out. At the top of both screens: four Raspberry Pi logos (one per CPU).
